FOUR-SENSOR WIRING AND EXPECTED OUTPUT
=====================================

Direct GPIO mode (included firmware default)
  Signal                        Input
  Light module AO               ESP32 GPIO32
  DHT11 DATA/S                  ESP32 GPIO27
  Sound module AO/ENV           ESP32 GPIO33
  IR obstacle module OUT/DO     ESP32 GPIO34
  Sensor power and ground       3.3 V for compatible modules, common GND

With ADS1115 (set USE_ADS1115 = true and reupload)
  ADS1115 VDD                   regulated 3.3 V
  ADS1115 GND                   ESP32 GND
  ADS1115 SDA                   GPIO21
  ADS1115 SCL                   GPIO22
  ADS1115 ADDR                  ESP32 GND (address 0x48)
  ADS1115 ALERT/RDY             leave open
  Light module AO               ADS1115 AIN0
  DHT11 DATA/S                  ESP32 GPIO27
  Sound module AO/ENV           ADS1115 AIN2
  IR obstacle module OUT/DO     ADS1115 AIN3
  ADS1115 AIN1                  unused
If the breakout lacks pull-ups, add 4.7 kΩ from SDA to 3.3 V and another
4.7 kΩ from SCL to 3.3 V. Check that the ADS1115 shares the ESP32 ground.

Use the sensor's printed pin labels. Connect a bare DHT11 with its grille
facing you and pins down as 3.3 V, DATA, unused, GND left-to-right. Add a
10 kΩ DATA-to-3.3 V pull-up to a bare sensor or a module lacking one.
For a bare LDR, wire 3.3 V -> LDR -> light input -> 10 kΩ -> GND.
All inputs must stay in the 0–3.3 V range.

1. Light: cover it or shine a light on it. The bare LDR divider listed above
   rises in brighter light; module polarity can differ.
2. DHT11: warm the case gently with your fingers. Temperature updates about
   every 2.5 seconds. Use Measure CH2 when the DHT11 label appears.
3. Sound: a clap changes the voltage on an envelope output. Raw microphone
   audio is limited by the ADC sampling rate. A digital output can instead
   show a threshold crossing.
4. IR: move your hand in front of it. Output switches between LOW and HIGH.
   On active-low modules, detection is the LOW state.

A disconnected DHT11 leaves three voltage traces active. The labels show
which signal each temporary channel contains. If you choose ADS1115 mode
and it does not respond, the DSO shows an ADS1115 wiring hint.

Original PWM experiment: flash the supplied PWM example separately and
restore the PWM LED/divider connections. The Python app still recognizes
its original serial packet format and applies its 100 Hz preset.
