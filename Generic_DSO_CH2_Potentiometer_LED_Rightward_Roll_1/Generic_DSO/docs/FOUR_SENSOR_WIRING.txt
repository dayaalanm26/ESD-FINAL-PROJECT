FOUR-CHANNEL WIRING AND EXPECTED OUTPUT
======================================

Direct ESP32 wiring (included firmware default, USE_ADS1115 = false)
  Light module AO               GPIO32 (CH1, volts)
  Potentiometer center/wiper    GPIO35 (CH2, volts)
  Sound module AO / ENV         GPIO33 (CH3, volts)
  IR obstacle module DO / OUT   GPIO34 (CH4, logic)

Connect the outer legs of a 10 kΩ pot to 3.3 V and GND. Connect compatible
module VCC to 3.3 V and all grounds to ESP32 GND. Use the same 3.3 V rail
as the sensors. With an external 3.3 V supply, join the grounds and leave
the regulator outputs separate. Keep all inputs inside 0–3.3 V.
For a bare LDR: 3.3 V -> LDR -> CH1 junction -> 10 kΩ -> GND.
Nominal wiper span is ground to supply. The ESP32 ADC can saturate near
the upper end, so use the waveform as a measured voltage reading.

LED brightness and physical probe
--------------------------------
  GPIO25 -> 1 kΩ resistor -> LED anode (+)
  LED cathode (-) -> ESP32 GND

GPIO25 produces 1 kHz PWM. Duty increases with CH2 voltage. The
firmware smooths the LED control; CH2 stays a direct voltage reading.
If the LED misses either endpoint, check the CH2 readings at the two
knob limits and adjust POT_OFF_V and POT_FULL_V in the sketch.
If necessary, set POWER_10K_POT_FROM_GPIO26 = true and wire the 10 kΩ
pot's high-side leg to GPIO26 instead of 3.3 V. GPIO26 is for this
pot alone. It must be free of other connections and power sources.

Physical scope CH1 probe tip goes to GPIO25 and its ground clip goes
to ESP32 GND. Begin at 500 µs/div, DC coupling and rising-edge trigger
near 1.65 V. Adjust probe attenuation in both the probe and the scope.

IR checks
---------
Use module DO / OUT and remove the old PWM divider from GPIO34.
Check its detection LED and adjust its trimmer if available. GPIO34
has no internal pull-up. An open-collector output lacking a pull-up
needs an external 10 kΩ to 3.3 V. CH4 IR Output plots the sampled DO
level: 0 is LOW and 1 is HIGH. The default active-low module pulls DO
LOW on detection. If yours goes HIGH on detection, set IR_ACTIVE_LOW
= false and reupload to update the IR status label.

To compare against the bench scope, probe the same DO/GPIO34 node and
use ESP32 GND for the probe clip. Use DC coupling. The Python chart
shows logic levels 0 and 1. The bench scope shows the pin voltage.

Optional ADS1115
----------------
Set USE_ADS1115 = true and reupload.
  ADS1115 VDD                   regulated 3.3 V
  ADS1115 GND                   ESP32 GND
  ADS1115 SDA                   GPIO21
  ADS1115 SCL                   GPIO22
  ADS1115 ADDR                  GND (address 0x48)
  ADS1115 ALERT / RDY           leave open
  Light AO                      ADS1115 AIN0
  Potentiometer center/wiper    ADS1115 AIN1
  Sound AO / ENV                ADS1115 AIN2
  IR DO / OUT                   ADS1115 AIN3
If the breakout has no I2C pull-ups, add 4.7 kΩ from SDA and SCL to 3.3 V.

Display and export
------------------
In Roll the newest data is at the left. Samples move to the right and
leave the viewport. Stop holds the current screen. The time axis shows
age. CSV export keeps rows in device-time order, from oldest to newest.
The board cadence, USB driver and computer determine the refresh.
