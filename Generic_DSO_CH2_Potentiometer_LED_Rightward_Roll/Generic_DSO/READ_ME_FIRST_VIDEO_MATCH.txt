GENERIC DSO — VIDEO-MATCHED 100 HZ PWM — SMOOTH EDITION

1. Double-click Generic_DSO\Start_DSO.bat after extracting the ZIP.
2. Choose your ESP32 COM port at 921600 baud and click Connect.
3. Press PWM 100 Hz (automatically selected on ESP32 PWM identification).
4. 5 ms/div; 2 V/div; CH1 rising trigger 1.65 V; DC; 1X.
5. Acquire > Display interpolation: PWM steps (measured).
6. Acquire > Tektronix dark signal screen: checked.
7. Verify CH1 gain ×1. Turn the potentiometer and watch duty change.

The light-themed UI and previous 1 Hz Tektronix preset are preserved.
Original unmodified project backup remains inside ORIGINAL_BACKUP/.

See Generic_DSO\VIDEO_REFERENCE_AND_SMOOTH_SETUP.md for evidence,
wiring, data-rate limitations and accurate verification status.
